Starry Chess — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated: October 24, 2026

Short version: Starry Chess does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no user account. Your game progress is kept only on your own device. The only outside service the App talks to is Apple Game Center, and only if you choose to use it.

1. No account, no personal data collected

Starry Chess does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information (name, email, phone number, location, etc.) to play. The App does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data to MAG or to any third party.

2. Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, Stars earned, unlocked Art Gallery cards, settings, and statistics are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. This data:

  • never leaves your device under normal use (no backend server, no cloud sync operated by MAG),
  • is only included in standard iOS/iCloud device backups if you have those enabled on your device — that backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by MAG,
  • is deleted automatically if you delete the App.

3. Game Center (Apple)

Starry Chess optionally uses Apple Game Center so you can post your Rush mode scores to a leaderboard and see how you compare to other players. Game Center is:

  • entirely optional — you can play the entire App, including Rush mode, without ever signing in to Game Center,
  • operated and controlled by Apple Inc., not by MAG. When you sign in to Game Center and submit a score, that data (such as your Game Center Player ID and your score) is handled under Apple's own privacy policy, available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

MAG does not receive, store, or have access to your Game Center account information beyond what Apple's GameKit framework displays inside the App (such as the leaderboard itself).

4. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs

As of this version (v1.0, verified against the shipped source code), Starry Chess does not include any third-party analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or tracking SDK of any kind (for example: no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no AdMob, no Facebook SDK, no Amplitude, no Mixpanel, no AppsFlyer, no Sentry/Crashlytics). The App does not show ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

If a future version of the App adds any such service, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the update will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

5. Children's privacy

Starry Chess does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects no personal data and requires no account, there is no personal data to request or delete for any user, child or adult.

6. Your choices

  • You can play the entire App, forever, without ever connecting to the internet or signing in to anything.
  • You can delete all locally stored game data at any time by deleting the App from your device.
  • You can choose not to sign in to Game Center; doing so only disables the optional Rush leaderboard feature.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
